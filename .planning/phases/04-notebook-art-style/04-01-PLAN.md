Structure:

**1. The cap.** State it as a number: **4**. Say that the number also lives in
`_sass/_tokens.scss` beside the TOKEN-06 caps (plan 04-02 writes it there), and that adding a
fifth mark means editing a stated number in two places rather than quietly appending a line.

**2. The four marks.** One section each. Every section carries a line beginning **exactly**

- **Section rule.** `Defence: it shows the reader where one group of work ends and the next
  begins.` Where: `.entry-group-title`, a hairline **under** the heading —
  `border-bottom: var(--rule-hairline) solid var(--rule-500)`, the same idiom already shipped

  the decorative `--rule-200`.
- **Margin note.** `Defence: it carries the open question or caveat behind the item it sits
  next to.` Where: `.entry-note`, an `<aside>` **sibling** of `.entry-body` inside `li.entry`,
  Research page only in this phase.

  `.entry-note::before`, drawn from `border-left` + `border-top` on a `content: ""`
  pseudo-element. Colour `--rule-200` (1.43:1) — and say plainly *why that is legitimate*:
  the bracket is meaningless on its own, so WCAG 1.4.11's 3:1 floor for "graphical objects
  needed to understand content" does not apply to it. Saying so is what makes this an argued
  position rather than an oversight.
- **Link underline.** `Defence: it marks a link without spending colour, which every warm ink
  accent on this site fails the 3:1 test against.` Shipped in Phase 2 on `.post article a`;
  listed here so it is part of the named vocabulary rather than a leftover. **No change to
  its values is in scope for this phase.**

**3. What is NOT a mark, and why — three decisions recorded, not three omissions.**

- **No hand-drawn mark.** PITFALLS recommends "one signature move, repeated with discipline"
  and names the hand-drawn link underline as the candidate. It was offered to the site owner
  and **declined**; the vocabulary is geometric throughout. This is a decided question. Do not
  reintroduce a drawn or irregular mark in a later phase without reopening it here.
- **The section rule reaches three pages, not seven.** Measured against the seven live pages:
  `.entry-group-title` appears on Academics (2), Research (2) and Activities (5). Scoping to
  `h2` instead would reach five pages and pull the **home page** in, which Phase 6 owns.
  Consequence stated plainly: **Further Reading and CV have no `h2` at all and will carry
  ground, type and texture but no mark whatsoever.** That is the honest result of "every mark
  names the content it serves" on pages that have no sections — not a miss, and Phase 8's
  screenshot count must not read it as one. Same precedent as Phase 3's `title-full`, where
  the absence of a selector was itself the decision.
